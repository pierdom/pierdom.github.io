Where I sit: industry, not academia

I am a business data leader, not a researcher. Since 2018 I have worked exclusively in commercial B2B/B2C SaaS product companies, in roles from Data Lead to Head of Data to Global VP of Data to Senior Director. My accountability is business outcomes: product decisions, revenue operations, executive and board reporting, and the teams that deliver them.

The research chapter is real, but it is history. My last research role ended in February 2018; my PhD (TU Wien) completed in 2015, and my publication record runs 2012–2018. I have not held an academic or research post in over eight years, and I am not looking for one.

What carries forward is the technical depth, not the job description. A PhD in data mining and machine learning is an operational advantage when you are building an AI-native data function — semantic layers, agentic access to governed data, decision science that survives contact with production. It is the reason I can lead this work credibly, not evidence that I do something else for a living. The research archive is kept for the record.
